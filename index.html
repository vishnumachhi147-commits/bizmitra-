Market check before launch

  • Customer: Kaun pay karega?
  • Problem: Kaunsi real problem solve hoti hai?
  • Competitors: 5 local/online alternatives note karein.
  • Pricing: 3 competitor prices compare karein.
  • Validation: 10 potential customers se baat karein.
  • Unit economics: Cost, price aur margin calculate karein.
Important: Idea ka “market demand” city, customer segment aur pricing se badalta hai. Budget, sales aur profit ke estimates planning ke liye hain; income ki guarantee nahi. Business decisions mein parent/guardian ya trusted adult ki help aur local rules ki checking zaroor karein.

Start karne ka simple roadmap

Bina bade investment ke pehle idea validate karein.

STEP 01

Problem choose karo

Apne area ya online audience ki ek specific problem identify karein.

STEP 02

Customer se baat

10–15 potential customers se needs, alternatives aur budget samjhein.

STEP 03

Small pilot

Demo, sample ya limited trial banayein. Feedback ke bina scale na karein.

STEP 04

Review & improve

Expenses, customer response aur repeat interest dekhkar next step decide karein.
